# Mixing Math and Pixels

After finishing Unit 1, we started off by learning about **Image Transformations** and its types. It was quite fun and interesting to learn about the different ways we can view and transform images in our computers. It also introduced us to the concepts of **Functions**. In math, I always thought functions were just _dry equations in a textbook or problem_. But as we started to learn about how images transform and how functions are involved in it, it became more than just another mathematics symbol. It started to look like a machine that takes a pixel and transforms it into something new. And when we start chaining those machines together, we get a **composite**. It gives us the ability to rotate, resize and blur images in one command rather than take 3 different steps. 

It was very challenging to learn the basics of functions and drawing its various graphs. Finding and solving for composite functions was also very tough. But at the end, I got the hang of things by spending more time studying. Looking back at it, the topics were quite easy. The only thing that was hard to grasp at the start was the _logic of solving such questions_. 

Shortly after this we started to work on optimization and its problems. This was easier compared to the previous topics as it was more logical and intuitive rather than learning topics from the basics. Solving these problems were fun as they were like solving puzzles. Learning the strategies to solve these problems. The most interesting types of problems I learned was the **Train Problems**. It was a mix of understanding graphs and numbers in real time to figure out the best possible time a train can take in a track. Overall, these were fun to solve and discuss with teammates.
