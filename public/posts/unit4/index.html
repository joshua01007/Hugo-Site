Typebot and Metabase Projects(Unit4)

May 11, 2026

Using New Tools

In Unit 4, I learned how to use 2 new tools in some degree. The unit started off very strange as we didn’t really get any indication that we moved on from Unit 3. We were suddenly handed some projects on one of our Tuesday classes and made to learn the tools from scratch, without our instructors help. This combined with the fact that we were nearing the end of the semester and were piled with other course work meant we also were going to have troubles in time management. But this was not the case. It was, in my opinion, one the more fun units in our course syllabus.

We started off by making a chatbot in the first of our two-assignment gauntlet. We made this chatbot in the Tybebot website. The process was quite simple and enjoyable. It was block based making the work beginner friendly and easy. The workload which I was scared about was not a big problem because we were given 1 week to work on our projects. The project being open-ended also meant that we could be creative with our projects and add our own twists.

Our second project was a Metabase dashboard project. This was also very easy and intuative. The website UI being user friendly added to my enjoyment as well. The project was that we were supposed to make a dashboard on Metabase with some pollution data that our instructor provided. The time problem as also avoided due to our instructor giving us 1 week to do it.

For both projects, doing them felt very easy and fun. The projects grabbed me in some way where I was doing them in my leisure. I some times picked the up the projects so that I could take a break from other course work and spent my free time watching youtube vidoes and tutorials in the net. So far, It was one of the best experiences I had working on our projects.
